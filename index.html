06 - Bid and ask
Multiplication keeps the sides
Synthetic range
bid = 1.1633 × 153.32 = 178.3571
ask = 1.1635 × 153.33 = 178.3995
The rule underneath
A side flips only when a quote is inverted. Multiplying inverts nothing, so bid pairs with bid. Dividing inverts the denominator, so the sides cross.
Here · multiply
bid × bid
ask × ask
Two European quotes · divide
bid ÷ ask
ask ÷ bid

11 - Classification
A hedge needs something to hedge
Hedge
Underlying exposure exists and is measured
Notional sized to that exposure
Instrument carries opposite sensitivity
Combined ∂V/∂S moves toward zero
Comerci
Peso revenue, peso costs, peso customers
Notional far beyond any dollar liability
Same direction as the existing position
Combined ∂V/∂S moved away from zero
The claim made in court
The company stated it had traded derivatives since the 1990s to hedge currency risk.
The instruments were hedging instruments. The application was not.

The structural point

13 - Governance
Treasury as a profit centre
What failed
No notional limit on treasury positions
Board without sight of aggregate exposure
Derivative gains booked as performance
Borrowing used to fund the speculation
Why it survived for years
The peso had been stable or strengthening. The position paid every quarter it was open, and a treasury that reports profit is not questioned.
Not an isolated failure
Cemex took comparable damage. In Brazil, Aracruz, Votorantim and Sadia lost roughly 4.9bn reais on the same trade.
